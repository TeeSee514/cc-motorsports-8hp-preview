From Car Details to a Written Build

The first conversation should be simple. C&C needs to understand the car and identify the transmission before confirming parts and pricing.

  1. 01

    Tell us about the car

    Start with the vehicle, engine combination, power goals, how it is driven, and what you want to change.

  2. 02

    Confirm the unit and combination

    Tag information, diagnostic identification, drivetrain layout, converter, calibration, and current condition fill in the mechanical picture.

  3. 03

    Discuss the build

    C&C can then discuss the parts that fit the job and put the work, price, and terms in writing.

  4. 04

    Confirm the quote

    The accepted quote should identify the parts, work, price, timing, terms, and any checks C&C confirms for that transmission.
