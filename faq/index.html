ZF 8HP FAQ

Questions 8HP Owners Actually Ask

Identification, clutch packages, TCU setup, cooling, converters, shipping, timing, and warranty—answered without making up a rating or fitment.

Before you contact C&C

Have the tag and car details ready.

A readable transmission tag turns a broad 8HP question into a useful conversation quickly.

Open the build checklist
01What does C&C offer for the ZF 8HP?+

C&C offers ZF 8HP builds using Dodson Motorsport transmission components. The build paths covered here are the 8HP70 and 8HP90 families.

02Which 8HP do I have?+

Send a clear tag photo with the year, make, model, drivetrain, and donor information. The vehicle name by itself may not identify the transmission.

03Can you tell me if my car is a candidate?+

Send the vehicle details and a tag photo. C&C can confirm whether the transmission and the car are a match for the work being discussed.

04How much power can an 8HP handle?+

There is no useful answer from horsepower alone. C&C also needs the torque curve, vehicle weight, tire, launch behavior, calibration, temperature, and intended use.

05Which clutch package do I need?+

Start with the transmission tag and how the car is used. C&C will identify the clutch package and any related parts in the quote.

06Does TCU tuning matter?+

Yes. Include the TCU software, calibration, shift strategy, and torque-management details you have.

07Do I need upgraded cooling?+

It depends on the car and how often it sees hard use. Send the current cooler setup and any temperature data you have.

08Do you work with AWD versions?+

Send the tag, vehicle, and drivetrain details. C&C needs to identify the unit before confirming what applies.

09Do you supply converters?+

Include the current converter and what you want the car to do. The quote will state whether converter work is part of the job.

10Can I ship my transmission?+

Contact C&C first. Do not ship a transmission until the shop confirms the job and gives you receiving instructions.

11How long does a build take?+

Ask for current timing with the quote. Parts availability and the condition of the transmission can change the schedule.

12What does the warranty cover?+

Read the warranty supplied with the quote before authorizing work, and make sure C&C knows how the car will be used.

Still have a question?

Tell C&C what the car is doing.

Include the tag, vehicle, engine, output, use, tire, controls, and any current symptoms.
