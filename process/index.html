Technical workflow

Eleven checkpoints. Six controlled phases.

The checkpoints are grouped by decision dependency rather than rendered as eleven interchangeable blocks.

010203040506
  1. 01

    Identify

    Establish the exact vehicle, unit, and intake evidence.

    1. 01

      Build request intake

      Collect the vehicle, output, use, condition, service, and contact information needed for an initial review.

      Proposed
    2. 02

      Application identity check

      Verify the exact vehicle and transmission identity from reliable identifiers rather than assuming interchangeability.

      Proposed
  2. 02

    Understand

    Read duty, torque delivery, condition, and operating severity together.

    1. 03

      Duty-cycle review

      Review street use, roll racing, drag use, launch frequency, tire, drivetrain, and intended operating severity.

      Proposed
    2. 04

      Power-delivery review

      Assess current and target wheel horsepower, wheel torque, fuel, forced induction, and calibration strategy together.

      Proposed
    3. 05

      Condition and evidence review

      Review symptoms, prior failures, diagnostics, dyno information, tag photos, and existing modifications supplied by the requester.

      Proposed
  3. 03

    Specify

    Develop the provisional direction, inspection plan, and supporting-system scope.

    1. 06

      Provisional package direction

      Identify a possible Street, Competition, or Max Effort path without treating the package name as final scope.

      Proposed
    2. 07

      Inspection and measurement plan

      Define the proposed inspection, measurement, acceptance, and documentation checkpoints for the exact unit.

      Proposed
    3. 08

      Component and supporting-system review

      Evaluate clutch direction and any application-dependent hubs, carrier, converter, overhaul, cooling, fluid, pan, or calibration needs.

      Proposed
  4. 04

    Authorize

    Put selected work, exclusions, responsibilities, and terms in writing.

    1. 09

      Written quote and exclusions

      Present the selected scope, assumptions, exclusions, responsibilities, commercial terms, and approval points before work begins.

      Proposed
  5. 05

    Build

    Proceed only through the final approved service workflow.

    1. 10

      Approved service workflow

      Proceed only after scope approval, using the final shop procedure, documentation standard, and change-authorization process.

      Proposed
  6. 06

    Verify

    Complete the approved checks and controlled handoff.

    1. 11

      Verification and handoff

      Complete the approved checks and provide the approved operating, calibration, installation, fluid, service, and warranty information.

Customer view

The same discipline in six clear steps.

The simpler customer path does not remove the technical review behind it.

  1. 01

    Submit the application

    Describe the exact vehicle, transmission, output, torque, use, condition, and goals.

  2. 02

    Supply identifiers

    Provide tag photos, diagnostic information, dyno data, and modification details where available.

  3. 03

    Receive a technical review

    C&C reviews fitment, duty cycle, condition, parts direction, supporting needs, and open questions.

  4. 04

    Review a written recommendation

    The proposed package, scope, exclusions, logistics, pricing, timing, and warranty terms are documented for approval.

  5. 05

    Authorize the final scope

    No service begins until the application, unit, commercial terms, and change process are approved.

  6. 06

    Complete verification and handoff

    Approved inspection, build, verification, installation, calibration, and service information accompany the finished work as applicable.
