Identify the Transmission Before Picking Parts

A clear tag photo and the vehicle details usually get the conversation moving. Swaps and donor units need enough history to connect the transmission to the car.

Prepare Build Details

Start at the case

Get a readable tag photo.

Wipe the tag clean, hold the camera square to it, and include every line. Add diagnostic identification or a part number if you have it.

  1. 01

    Photograph the full tag

    Use good light and keep the numbers sharp enough to zoom in.

  2. 02

    Describe where the unit came from

    Say whether it is original to the car, already swapped, or still in a donor.

  3. 03

    Confirm the drivetrain

    Include RWD or AWD and any transfer case or driveline changes.

The rest of the review

The unit has to match the car.

Once the transmission is identified, these are the details that help C&C decide whether the current unit and proposed use make sense together.

How torque arrives

Engine, turbo or supercharger setup, fuel, current output, target output, and the shape of the torque curve.

What the car puts against it

Vehicle weight, tire, traction, launch method, converter, and how often the car repeats a hard pass.

How it is controlled

TCU strategy, calibration, torque management, shift behavior, and any known controller limits.

What condition it is in

Mileage, service history, slip, flare, harsh shifts, fault codes, fluid condition, and previous work.

Have the tag

Send the build details with it.

Include the vehicle, drivetrain, donor history, engine setup, use, current problems, and what you want the car to do.
