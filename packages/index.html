Build direction / not horsepower stages

Choose the Build Direction From How the Car Is Actually Used

Street, Competition, and Max Effort describe where the car lives and how it loads the transmission. They do not prescribe a fixed parts package.

Start Your 8HP Build
APPLICATION REVIEW REQUIRED

Build direction describes use. It does not replace application review.

C&C determines the parts after reviewing the transmission and complete vehicle.

Three operating profiles

Start with the description that sounds like the car.

The tag, torque delivery, mass, grip, launch behavior, controls, cooling, condition, and actual use settle the recommendation.

  1. 01

    Modified vehicles that still accumulate road miles

    Street

    For a car that has to cruise, commute, and make clean pulls while staying useful on the road.

    Priorities

    • Street behavior
    • Temperature
    • Repeatability
    • Occasional launches
    Typical use
    Road miles, highway pulls, occasional launches
    Matters most
    Street behavior, temperature, repeatability
    C&C looks at
    Condition, tire, converter, calibration, and cooling
    Supporting setup
    Service history, converter behavior, TCU setup, and cooler layout
  2. 02

    Street vehicles that regularly roll race, drag race, or make hard passes

    Competition

    For a car that still sees the street but repeats hard shifts, launches, or race use often enough for recovery to matter.

    Priorities

    • Grip
    • Launch load
    • Repeated hard shifts
    • Heat recovery
    Typical use
    Roll racing, drag passes, repeated hard shifts
    Matters most
    Grip, launch load, repeat heat
    C&C looks at
    Tire, weight, launch method, TCU setup, and condition
    Supporting setup
    Cooling, converter, torque management, and between-run temperature
  3. 03

    Dedicated or near-dedicated race use

    Max Effort

    For a car built around the pass, where the transmission, driveline, controls, tire, and event service plan all matter.

    Priorities

    • High-grip launches
    • Vehicle mass
    • Repeated passes
    • Complete driveline
    • Service between events
    Typical use
    High-grip launches, repeated passes, race duty
    Matters most
    The hit, vehicle mass, heat, and service between events
    C&C looks at
    Transmission, driveline, launch strategy, tire, data, and condition
    Supporting setup
    Converter, cooling, calibration, and inspection between events
