8HP70 Builds

The 8HP70 is part of ZF's eight-speed automatic family. C&C starts with the transmission tag, the car, and how it is used before choosing parts.

Why owners look beyond a stock 8HP70

Added torque, a harder tire, more frequent launches, extra heat, or an existing problem can change what the transmission needs. A street car and a drag car can ask very different things of the same transmission family.

C&C looks at the transmission as part of the car: engine, torque curve, weight, tire, launch behavior, converter, TCU calibration, cooling, and current condition.

Dodson Motorsport product family

Clutch kits, converter upgrades, hubs, and overhaul parts

Dodson publishes an 8HP65/70/75/76 upgrade range that includes clutch kits, high-stall converter upgrades, billet hub upgrades, overhaul kits, and an assembly toolkit. C&C uses the exact unit details to determine what is relevant.

What C&C looks at

  • Engine combination and where the torque comes in
  • Vehicle weight, drivetrain, tire, and available grip
  • Street use, hard pulls, drag use, and launch frequency
  • Converter, TCU calibration, cooling, and shift behavior
  • Fluid condition, faults, prior failures, and previous work

What to have ready

  • A clear transmission tag photo
  • Part number or diagnostic identification, if available
  • Vehicle and engine details
  • Current problems and service history
  • Power goals and an honest description of how the car is used
