ZF 8HP build levels

How the Car Will Use the Transmission

The three levels describe how the car is used. C&C sets the parts after reviewing the unit, torque delivery, tire, control, cooling, and condition.

Prepare Build Details

Build levels

Street, Competition, and Max Effort

Each level describes a use pattern. C&C still names the parts after reviewing the car and transmission.

  1. 01

    Modified street cars

    C&C Street

    For cars that still commute, cruise, and make highway pulls. Street behavior and temperature stay in the decision.

    Use
    Daily miles, highway pulls, occasional launches
    Load
    Added torque, occasional launches, sustained road use
    Review
    Unit condition, tire, calibration, cooling, converter
  2. 02

    Street cars that race regularly

    C&C Competition

    For street cars that see regular roll or drag use. Grip, launch load, and back to back runs carry more weight.

    Use
    Roll racing, drag use, repeated hard shifts
    Load
    Sticky tires, repeated hard shifts, more frequent launches
    Review
    Launch behavior, tire, weight, repeat use, TCU setup
  3. 03

    Dedicated or near dedicated race cars

    C&C Max Effort

    For cars built around racing. The review starts with the complete driveline and how the car is serviced between events.

    Use
    High grip, repeated launches, race duty
    Load
    Vehicle mass, tire, launch method, repeat heat
    Review
    Complete driveline, current unit, service between events

What can move the recommendation

Four details that change a build quickly

Grip
A sticky tire and hard launch send more of the hit into the driveline.
Weight
Vehicle mass changes how hard the transmission has to accelerate the car.
Repeat use
Back to back passes change the temperature and recovery picture.
Current damage
Slip, flare, faults, or previous work can add repair needs before upgrades.
