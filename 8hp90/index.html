What changes the job

What the transmission has to live with

Peak power matters. The way the car delivers and repeats that load can matter just as much.

  1. 01

    Torque delivery

    Current power, target power, boost response, and the torque curve tell C&C more than a peak horsepower number by itself.

  2. 02

    Weight and grip

    A heavy car on a prepared surface asks a different question than a lighter car that rolls into power on the highway.

  3. 03

    Launch frequency

    A few street pulls and repeated hard launches create different load and temperature patterns. Say how the car is actually used.

  4. 04

    Control and condition

    TCU calibration, converter, cooling, faults, fluid condition, mileage, and previous work all belong in the first review.

Billet P2 planetary carrier
