Good / Better / Best

Three Outcomes. No Universal Parts List.

Package names organize a conversation about intended use. Exact parts, capacity, scope, price, timing, warranty, and acceptance still come from the application review and written quote.

Good

C&C Street

A provisional direction for modified street vehicles that prioritize drivability, predictable road use, and an application-matched scope.

Intended use
Modified street driving with performance use reviewed against the exact vehicle, calibration, tire, torque, and launch profile.
Provisional clutch direction
Appropriate Dodson SUPERSTOCK clutch system, subject to exact transmission identification and C&C approval.
Major scope direction
  • Incoming inspection and identification plan
  • Application review before parts selection
  • Provisional clutch-system direction
  • Written quote defining selected work and exclusions
Current exclusions
  • No public power or torque capacity
  • No assumed converter, hub, carrier, pan, fluid, or calibration inclusion
  • No published price, timing, or warranty until approved
Price
Quoted by application

Better

C&C Competition

A provisional direction for serious high-power street, roll-racing, and occasional drag applications requiring deeper duty-cycle review.

Intended use
Mixed high-power street and competition use where launch frequency, tire, torque delivery, cooling, and calibration materially affect the scope.
Provisional clutch direction
Appropriate Dodson SPORTSMAN'S clutch system, subject to exact transmission identification and C&C approval.
Major scope direction
  • Detailed use and torque-delivery review
  • Inspection-led supporting-part assessment
  • Provisional competition clutch direction
  • Application quote defining required supporting work
Current exclusions
  • No universal race-use acceptance
  • No assumed hub, carrier, converter, cooling, or TCU work
  • No published capacity, price, timing, or warranty until approved
Price
Quoted by application

Best

C&C Max Effort

An application-reviewed direction for serious drag, roll-racing, or extreme-power systems where the full combination must be assessed.

Intended use
High-severity programs reviewed individually, including launch strategy, tire, torque curve, vehicle configuration, supporting systems, and service plan.
Provisional clutch direction
SPORTSMAN'S PLUS or PROMAX direction; required supporting parts depend on the exact variant and use.
Major scope direction
  • Full application and operating-severity review
  • Exact-variant component-path assessment
  • Supporting-part, converter, cooling, and calibration review
  • Written scope with explicit limits and customer responsibilities
Current exclusions
  • No unlimited-power or guaranteed-capacity claim
  • No package selection from horsepower alone
  • No published capacity, price, timing, or warranty until approved
