Site Terms

These pages explain C&C’s ZF 8HP build paths and help owners prepare for a real shop conversation without creating a quote or fitment promise.

Transmission information

A family name does not identify every variant or prove vehicle fitment. C&C confirms the transmission and parts for the job.

Job information

The written quote controls parts, price, timing, shipping, and warranty. Contact C&C before shipping a transmission or buying parts for a build.

Local Build Brief

The Build Brief prepares copy inside the current browser tab. It does not submit a request, reserve work, create an account, or save a job.

Dodson references

Dodson links describe the manufacturer’s published component range. They do not establish a complete-transmission rating or a guaranteed result for a car.
