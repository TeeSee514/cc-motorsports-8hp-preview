What changes the build

Five things that shape the build

C&C needs to know how the car makes torque, how much grip it has, how often it launches, and what controls the shifts.

  1. 01

    The exact 8HP

    Variant, tag information, part number, donor history, and whether the unit is RWD or AWD.

  2. 02

    How torque arrives

    Engine, turbo or supercharger setup, fuel, current output, target output, and the shape of the torque curve.

  3. 03

    What the car puts against it

    Vehicle weight, tire, traction, launch method, converter, and how often the car repeats a hard pass.

  4. 04

    How it is controlled

    TCU strategy, calibration, torque management, shift behavior, and any known controller limits.

  5. 05

    What condition it is in

    Mileage, service history, slip, flare, harsh shifts, fault codes, fluid condition, and previous work.

Build starting points

Start with how the car is used

Street covers road use. Competition covers regular racing. Max Effort starts with dedicated race duty.

  1. 01

    Modified street cars

    C&C Street

    For cars that still commute, cruise, and make highway pulls. Street behavior and temperature stay in the decision.

    Use
    Daily miles, highway pulls, occasional launches
  2. 02

    Street cars that race regularly

    C&C Competition

    For street cars that see regular roll or drag use. Grip, launch load, and back to back runs carry more weight.

    Use
    Roll racing, drag use, repeated hard shifts
  3. 03

    Dedicated or near dedicated race cars

    C&C Max Effort

    For cars built around racing. The review starts with the complete driveline and how the car is serviced between events.

    Use
    High grip, repeated launches, race duty

Common questions

Straight answers before you call.

Read every answer
01What does C&C offer for the ZF 8HP?+

ZF 8HP builds available using Dodson Motorsport transmission components. This site covers the 8HP70 and 8HP90 families and the details C&C needs before discussing parts for a job.

02Which 8HP do I have?+

Send a clear photo of the transmission tag along with the year, make, model, drivetrain, and any donor information. The vehicle model alone may not identify the exact unit.

03Can you tell me if my car is a candidate?+

C&C needs the vehicle details and transmission tag before answering. This site does not publish a vehicle fitment list, and swaps or donor transmissions need their history documented.

04How much power can an 8HP handle?+

Power alone does not answer it. C&C also needs torque delivery, vehicle weight, tire, launch behavior, calibration, temperature, and use before discussing a build.
