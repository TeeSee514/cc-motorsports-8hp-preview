Full comparison

Three different jobs for an 8HP.

These are build directions, not fixed horsepower tiers or boxed parts packages.

  1. 01

    Modified cars that still rack up road miles

    Street

    For a car that has to cruise, commute, and make clean pulls without turning every drive into race-car maintenance.

    Typical use
    Daily driving, highway pulls, occasional launches
    Matters most
    Street manners, temperature, repeatability
    C&C looks at
    Current condition, tire, converter, calibration, cooling
    Supporting setup
    Converter behavior, TCU setup, cooling, and service history
  2. 02

    Street cars that see regular race use

    Competition

    For cars that still see the street but regularly roll race, drag race, or make back-to-back hard passes.

    Typical use
    Roll racing, drag passes, repeated hard shifts
    Matters most
    Grip, launch load, repeat heat
    C&C looks at
    Tire, weight, launch method, TCU setup, current condition
    Supporting setup
    Cooling, converter, torque management, between-run temperature
  3. 03

    Dedicated or near-dedicated race cars

    Max Effort

    For a car built around the pass. The transmission, driveline, controls, tire, and event service plan all matter.

    Typical use
    High-grip launches, repeated passes, race duty
    Matters most
    The hit, vehicle mass, heat, service between events
    C&C looks at
    Transmission, driveline, launch strategy, tire, data, condition
    Supporting setup
    Converter, cooling, calibration, inspection between events

What moves the build

Four details can change the direction fast.

Grip
A sticky tire and hard launch send more of the hit into the driveline.
Weight
Vehicle mass changes how hard the transmission has to accelerate the car.
Repeat use
Back-to-back passes change the heat and recovery picture.
Condition
Slip, flare, faults, or previous work can add repair needs first.
