Choose the Right 8HP Build for the Car

C&C Street, Competition, and Max Effort sort the conversation by how the car is used. The exact transmission and setup determine the parts.

Modified street cars

C&C Street

For a car that spends most of its time on the road and still needs to handle added power cleanly.

Use levelRoad use with occasional hard pulls
  • Road manners and normal driving
  • Current condition and service history
  • Torque delivery, tire, calibration, and cooling

High-power street and competition cars

C&C Competition

For combinations that see harder use, more grip, and more repeated load than a typical street car.

Use levelFrequent hard pulls with roll or drag use
  • How often the car is raced
  • Launch behavior and available traction
  • Converter, TCU calibration, cooling, and driveline setup

Serious race combinations

C&C Max Effort

For cars where the transmission has to be considered as part of the complete race combination.

Use levelHigh-grip, repeated, high-severity use
  • Vehicle weight, tire, and launch strategy
  • Torque curve and shift behavior
  • The full transmission, converter, control, and cooling picture
