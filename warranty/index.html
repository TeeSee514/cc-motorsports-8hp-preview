Warranty documentation

Coverage must be written before work is authorized.

C&C should not ask a customer to infer coverage from a package name, component page, manufacturer rating, or quote discussion. The terms that apply must be specific, complete, and accepted before service.

Documentation standard

Ambiguity is not coverage.

A serious build deserves a written document that defines what applies, what does not, how the transmission must be operated and serviced, and how a claim is handled.

  1. 01

    Coverage

    Scope, covered party, duration, start date, and application-specific conditions.

    Required
  2. 02

    Operating conditions

    Street, roll-racing, drag, dyno, competition, output, torque, fuel, calibration, tire, and cooling treatment.

    Required
  3. 03

    Installation and service

    Commissioning, fluid, filter, pan, maintenance, inspection, diagnostics, and records.

    Required
  4. 04

    Exclusions

    Wear, misuse, overheating, contamination, collateral damage, and nonapproved changes.

    Required
  5. 05

    Claims

    Notice, authorization, removal, installation, labor, freight, packaging, insurance, and risk allocation.

    Required
  6. 06

    Remedy

    Repair, replacement, credit, refund, transfer, limitations, component terms, and applicable law.

    Required

Current boundary

Do not authorize work from this page.

Until written terms are approved, warranty remains unresolved. A future quote must identify the terms that actually apply to the accepted application and service.

Capacity boundary: A manufacturer component rating or component warranty is not a complete C&C transmission rating, capacity guarantee, or C&C warranty.
