DEVELOPMENT BOUNDARY

Local review only · Updated August 12, 2026

1. Development status

The site is local-only, noindex by default, and not configured for production customer intake. Content marked “CHRIS TO CONFIRM” is deliberately unresolved and blocks production readiness.

2. No offer, quote, or authorization

Package names, descriptions, comparison values, compatibility states, process steps, and form choices do not create an offer, estimate, accepted application, work order, appointment, deadline, or authorization. Pricing is quoted by application only after C&C approves a written workflow.

3. Application and compatibility

No initial compatibility entry is Confirmed. “Requires review” is a candidate intake state, not fitment, interchangeability, parts availability, service acceptance, or performance approval. “Not currently offered” may be a generic state and does not describe a specific real vehicle unless C&C says so in writing.

4. Package and component direction

C&C Street, C&C Competition, and C&C Max Effort are provisional package names. No component is represented as Included. Final clutch system, hubs, carrier, converter, overhaul components, filter, pan, fluid, cooling, calibration, core, installation, shipping, and service scope remain application dependent.

ZF 8HP builds available using Dodson Motorsport transmission components. The wording is provisional, and manufacturer product availability does not establish C&C package inclusion or interchangeability.

5. Ratings and outcomes

Component manufacturer ratings apply to the manufacturer’s component claim and conditions. They do not establish complete C&C build capacity, vehicle performance, service life, or a guarantee. This site publishes no final C&C horsepower or torque rating, customer result, testimonial, or unsupported quality claim.

6. Warranty and commercial terms

No public warranty, lead time, starting price, inspection fee, deposit, cancellation rule, core term, freight allocation, tax treatment, or remedy has been approved. Any future transaction must be controlled by the written terms C&C identifies for that application.

7. Staging form

Use only fictional or synthetic test data. A valid enabled dry-run is discarded and sends no email or notification. The form does not create a request in C&C systems, reserve service, or promise a response.

8. Intellectual property and supplied evidence

No logo image, customer image, testimonial, technical drawing, generated mechanical art, or unapproved project result is used. Any future website image must depict a genuine relevant transmission or verified C&C-sponsored car and have documented public-domain status before publication.

9. Production legal terms

Governing law, venue, limitations, disclaimers, intellectual-property notices, user-submission rights, accessibility contact, and change notice remain unresolved.
