Transmission identification

Find Your ZF 8HP

The badge on the trunk does not identify every transmission variant. A clean tag photo and the unit’s history give C&C a much better start.

Start Your Build

Start at the case

Get one readable tag photo.

Wipe the tag clean, hold the camera square to it, and include every line. Add diagnostic identification or a part number if you have it.

  1. 01

    Photograph the full tag

    Keep the numbers sharp enough to read without guessing.

  2. 02

    Say where the unit came from

    Original to the car, already swapped, or still in a donor.

  3. 03

    Confirm the drivetrain

    Include RWD or AWD and any transfer-case or driveline changes.

After identification

Match the transmission to the car.

These details tell C&C what the unit has to live with once the tag is known.

Torque

Engine, boost setup, fuel, current output, target output, and torque curve.

Vehicle

Weight, drivetrain, gearing, swap details, and the rest of the driveline.

Traction

Tire, surface, launch method, and how hard the car can hit the transmission.

Use

Street miles, roll racing, drag racing, launch frequency, and repeat passes.

Control

TCU software, calibration, torque management, converter, and shift behavior.

Condition

Mileage, service history, slip, flare, fault codes, fluid, and previous work.

Have the tag?

Add the car and the goal.

Include the vehicle, drivetrain, donor history, engine setup, tire, use, current problems, and what you want the car to do.
