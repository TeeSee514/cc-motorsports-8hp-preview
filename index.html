Build direction

Street. Competition. Max Effort.

Pick the description that sounds like your car. C&C uses the real combination to settle the parts.

  1. 01

    Modified cars that still rack up road miles

    Street

    For a car that has to cruise, commute, and make clean pulls without turning every drive into race-car maintenance.

    Typical use
    Daily driving, highway pulls, occasional launches
    Matters most
    Street manners, temperature, repeatability
  2. 02

    Street cars that see regular race use

    Competition

    For cars that still see the street but regularly roll race, drag race, or make back-to-back hard passes.

    Typical use
    Roll racing, drag passes, repeated hard shifts
    Matters most
    Grip, launch load, repeat heat
  3. 03

    Dedicated or near-dedicated race cars

    Max Effort

    For a car built around the pass. The transmission, driveline, controls, tire, and event service plan all matter.

    Typical use
    High-grip launches, repeated passes, race duty
    Matters most
    The hit, vehicle mass, heat, service between events

What shapes the build

Horsepower is one line in the story.

  1. 01Transmission

    Tag, part number, variant, donor history, and RWD or AWD.

  2. 02Torque

    Engine, boost setup, fuel, current output, target output, and torque curve.

  3. 03Vehicle

    Weight, drivetrain, gearing, swap details, and the rest of the driveline.

  4. 04Traction

    Tire, surface, launch method, and how hard the car can hit the transmission.

  5. 05Use

    Street miles, roll racing, drag racing, launch frequency, and repeat passes.

  6. 06Control

    TCU software, calibration, torque management, converter, and shift behavior.

  7. 07Condition

    Mileage, service history, slip, flare, fault codes, fluid, and previous work.

Before you contact the shop

Straight answers to the common questions.

Read the full FAQ
01What does C&C offer for the ZF 8HP?+

C&C offers ZF 8HP builds using Dodson Motorsport transmission components. The build paths covered here are the 8HP70 and 8HP90 families.

02Which 8HP do I have?+

Send a clear tag photo with the year, make, model, drivetrain, and donor information. The vehicle name by itself may not identify the transmission.

03Can you tell me if my car is a candidate?+

Send the vehicle details and a tag photo. C&C can confirm whether the transmission and the car are a match for the work being discussed.

04How much power can an 8HP handle?+

There is no useful answer from horsepower alone. C&C also needs the torque curve, vehicle weight, tire, launch behavior, calibration, temperature, and intended use.

05Which clutch package do I need?+

Start with the transmission tag and how the car is used. C&C will identify the clutch package and any related parts in the quote.
