ZF 8HP questions

Questions 8HP Owners Actually Ask

These answers cover identification, build selection, calibration, cooling, converters, shipping, timing, and warranty.

Before you contact C&C

Have the tag and car details ready.

A readable transmission tag is the fastest way to turn a broad 8HP question into a useful conversation.

01What does C&C offer for the ZF 8HP?+

ZF 8HP builds available using Dodson Motorsport transmission components. This site covers the 8HP70 and 8HP90 families and the details C&C needs before discussing parts for a job.

02Which 8HP do I have?+

Send a clear photo of the transmission tag along with the year, make, model, drivetrain, and any donor information. The vehicle model alone may not identify the exact unit.

03Can you tell me if my car is a candidate?+

C&C needs the vehicle details and transmission tag before answering. This site does not publish a vehicle fitment list, and swaps or donor transmissions need their history documented.

04How much power can an 8HP handle?+

Power alone does not answer it. C&C also needs torque delivery, vehicle weight, tire, launch behavior, calibration, temperature, and use before discussing a build.

05Which clutch package do I need?+

Start with the exact transmission and how the car is used. C&C will name the clutch package and other parts that apply in the written quote.

06Does TCU tuning matter?+

C&C needs the TCU details before recommending a build. Include the software, calibration, shift strategy, and torque management information you have.

07Do I need upgraded cooling?+

That depends on the car and how often it sees hard use. Send the current cooler setup and any temperature data you have so C&C can include cooling in the review.

08Do you work with AWD versions?+

Send the tag and complete vehicle details. C&C needs the exact transmission and drivetrain before confirming what applies.

09Do you supply converters?+

Include the current converter and what you want from the car. The quote will state whether converter work is part of the job.

10Can I ship my transmission?+

Do not ship anything until C&C confirms the job and gives you receiving instructions.

11How long does a build take?+

Ask for current timing with the quote. Parts availability and what the transmission needs can change the schedule.

12What does the warranty cover?+

C&C should include the warranty that applies with the quote. Read it before authorizing work and make sure C&C knows how the car will be used.
