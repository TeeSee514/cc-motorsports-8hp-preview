Questions 8HP Owners Ask Before the Quote

Identification, capacity questions, clutch packages, TCU calibration, cooling, converters, shipping, timing, and warranty—answered without inventing a rating or fitment.

Before you contact C&C

Have the tag and car details ready.

A readable transmission tag turns a broad 8HP question into a useful conversation quickly.

Create your Build Brief
01Which 8HP do I have?+

Send a clear tag photo with the vehicle year, make, model, drivetrain, and donor information. The vehicle name alone may not identify the exact transmission.

02How much power can an 8HP handle?+

Horsepower alone is not a build sheet. C&C also needs the torque curve, vehicle weight, tire, launch behavior, calibration, cooling, condition, and intended use.

03Which clutch package do I need?+

Start with the transmission tag and actual use. C&C identifies the clutch package and related parts after reviewing the complete combination.

04Does TCU calibration matter?+

Yes. Include the TCU software, calibration, shift strategy, torque-management details, and converter commands you have.

05Can I ship my transmission?+

Contact C&C first. Do not ship a transmission until the shop confirms the job and provides receiving instructions.

06What does C&C offer for the ZF 8HP?+

ZF 8HP builds available using Dodson Motorsport transmission components. This microsite covers the 8HP70 and 8HP90 family paths.

07Can C&C tell me if my car is a candidate?+

Send the vehicle details and a tag photo. C&C can identify the unit and confirm what applies to the work being discussed.

08Do I need upgraded cooling?+

That depends on the combination and repeat use. Send the current cooler setup and any temperature information you have.

09Does C&C work with AWD versions?+

Send the tag, vehicle, and drivetrain details. C&C needs to identify the unit before confirming what applies.

10Does C&C supply converters?+

Include the current converter and what you want the car to do. The written quote will state whether converter work is part of the job.

11How long does a build take?+

Ask for current timing with the written quote. Parts availability and transmission condition can change the schedule.

12What does the warranty cover?+

Read the warranty supplied with the written quote before authorizing work, and make sure C&C knows how the car will be used.

Still have a question?

Tell C&C what the car is doing.

Include the tag, vehicle, engine, output, use, tire, controls, and any current symptoms.
