A useful first message has five things:

  1. 01Vehicle
  2. 02Tag photo
  3. 03Engine + output
  4. 04Use + tire
  5. 05Current problem
  1. 01You and the car
  2. 02Transmission
  3. 03Powertrain
  4. 04How the car is used
  5. 05Controls, cooling, and current problems
  1. 01

    You and the car

    Start with enough to identify the car and reach you.

    • Name and email
    • Phone and preferred contact methodOptional
    • Year, make, and model
    • Chassis codeOptional
    • RWD or AWD
    • Vehicle weightOptional
  2. 02

    Transmission

    A family name is a start. The tag tells C&C what is actually in front of you.

    • Clear transmission tag photo
    • Transmission model, if knownOptional
    • Part number or diagnostic identificationOptional
    • Original unit, donor transmission, or swap
    • Mileage and service historyOptional

    Photograph the full tag square to the camera and close enough to read every line.

  3. 03

    Powertrain

    Tell C&C what makes the torque now and what you plan to change.

    • Engine combination
    • Turbo or supercharger setup
    • Fuel
    • Current and target horsepower
    • Current and target torqueOptional
    • Torque curve or boost responseOptional
    • Dyno sheet or build sheetOptional
  4. 04

    How the car is used

    Describe the use plainly. A highway pull and a hard launch load the transmission differently.

    • Street, roll race, drag race, road course, or mixed use
    • Tire type and size
    • Launch method and frequency
    • How many hard passes or pulls the car repeatsOptional
  5. 05

    Controls, cooling, and current problems

    Finish with the details that often change the parts conversation.

    • Converter detailsOptional
    • TCU software or transmission control strategy
    • Cooling changes or current cooler setupOptional
    • Slip, flare, harsh shifts, fault codes, or noiseOptional
    • Previous transmission workOptional
    • Anything unusual about the swap or drivelineOptional
    • What you want the car to do better

Ready to start

You do not need every optional detail.

A vehicle summary, readable tag photo, engine setup, intended use, and current problem are enough to open the conversation.
