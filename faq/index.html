Questions About ZF 8HP Builds

The short answer is often that the exact transmission and car matter. These answers explain which details change the result.

What does C&C offer for ZF 8HP transmissions?+

ZF 8HP builds are available using Dodson Motorsport transmission components. The exact build depends on the unit and the car around it.

How do I identify my exact transmission?+

Start with a clear tag photo, any part numbers, and the diagnostic identification if you have it. Include donor information when the transmission did not come in the car.

Will an 8HP fit my car?+

That depends on the exact transmission and combination. Send the tag information and vehicle details and C&C can tell you what applies.

Which C&C build should I choose?+

Street, Competition, and Max Effort describe how the car is used. The final choice comes after C&C reviews the transmission, torque curve, tire, weight, launches, controls, cooling, and condition.

How much power can a built 8HP hold?+

C&C does not publish one complete-transmission rating here. The exact unit, torque delivery, vehicle, grip, calibration, temperature, and use all affect the answer.

Does every build use the same parts?+

No. Final parts are listed in the written quote for the exact transmission and car.

Are Dodson components available?+

Yes. ZF 8HP builds are available using Dodson Motorsport transmission components. The quote confirms which parts apply.

Is a torque converter part of the build?+

That depends on the converter, the transmission, and how the car is used. It must be confirmed in the written quote.

Will I need TCU calibration or more cooling?+

Possibly. C&C needs the current calibration, converter, cooling setup, power delivery, and intended use before answering.

What does a build cost?+

Pricing depends on the exact unit, its condition, and the work it needs. Parts and pricing are confirmed in writing.

How long will a build take?+

That depends on the transmission, inspection findings, parts, and current schedule. Confirm timing with C&C in writing.

What warranty applies?+

Confirm the warranty terms in writing before authorizing work. This preview does not set coverage, exclusions, or a claim process.
