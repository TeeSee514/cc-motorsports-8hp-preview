The core difference

Horsepower is not the build sheet.

Peak power matters. So do vehicle mass, torque delivery, tire, launch behavior, control strategy, cooling, repeat use, and current transmission condition. C&C starts with the complete combination.

    Weight, drivetrain, gearing, swap details, and the rest of the driveline.

  4. 04

    Cooler setup, temperature information, recovery time, and back-to-back use.

  8. 08

    Current condition

    Mileage, service history, slip, flare, fault codes, fluid, and previous work.

Build direction

Street. Competition. Max Effort.

These are use profiles, not generic horsepower stages. The actual parts are selected after C&C reviews the transmission and complete vehicle.

  1. 01

    Modified vehicles that still accumulate road miles

    Street

    For a car that has to cruise, commute, and make clean pulls while staying useful on the road.

    Priorities

    • Street behavior
    • Temperature
    • Repeatability
    • Occasional launches
    Typical use
    Road miles, highway pulls, occasional launches
    Matters most
    Street behavior, temperature, repeatability
  2. 02

    Street vehicles that regularly roll race, drag race, or make hard passes

    Competition

    For a car that still sees the street but repeats hard shifts, launches, or race use often enough for recovery to matter.

    Priorities

    • Grip
    • Launch load
    • Repeated hard shifts
    • Heat recovery
    Typical use
    Roll racing, drag passes, repeated hard shifts
    Matters most
    Grip, launch load, repeat heat
  3. 03

    Dedicated or near-dedicated race use

    Max Effort

    For a car built around the pass, where the transmission, driveline, controls, tire, and event service plan all matter.

    Priorities

    • High-grip launches
    • Vehicle mass
    • Repeated passes
    • Complete driveline
    • Service between events
    Typical use
    High-grip launches, repeated passes, race duty
    Matters most
    The hit, vehicle mass, heat, and service between events

Specification story

The build starts before the transmission comes apart.

Each fact changes the load case C&C is trying to understand.

ZF 8HPAPPLICATION SPECIFIED70 / 90
  1. 01

    Transmission identity

    The family name starts the conversation. The tag and part number identify the unit in front of C&C.

  2. 02

    Engine and torque delivery

    Peak output does not show how quickly torque arrives or how hard the converter and clutch elements are loaded.

  3. 03

    Weight and traction

    Vehicle mass and available grip decide how much of the hit travels through the transmission instead of the tire.

  4. 04

    Launch behavior

    A highway pull, a foot-brake launch, and a high-grip launch create different load events.

  5. 05

    TCU strategy

    Calibration, shift pressure, torque management, and converter commands belong in the mechanical conversation.

  6. 06

    Cooling

    The cooler layout and time between hard uses shape the repeat-temperature picture.

  7. 07

    Current condition

    Slip, flare, faults, fluid condition, and previous work can change the job before upgrades are selected.

  8. 08

    Actual use

    Street miles, roll racing, drag racing, and repeated passes define what the transmission must keep doing.

High-intent FAQ

Straight answers before you contact the shop.

Read the full FAQ
01Which 8HP do I have?+

Send a clear tag photo with the vehicle year, make, model, drivetrain, and donor information. The vehicle name alone may not identify the exact transmission.

02How much power can an 8HP handle?+

Horsepower alone is not a build sheet. C&C also needs the torque curve, vehicle weight, tire, launch behavior, calibration, cooling, condition, and intended use.

03Which clutch package do I need?+

Start with the transmission tag and actual use. C&C identifies the clutch package and related parts after reviewing the complete combination.

04Does TCU calibration matter?+

Yes. Include the TCU software, calibration, shift strategy, torque-management details, and converter commands you have.

05Can I ship my transmission?+

Contact C&C first. Do not ship a transmission until the shop confirms the job and provides receiving instructions.
