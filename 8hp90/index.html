ZF eight-speed automatic

8HP90 Builds

The 8HP90 belongs to ZF's eight-speed automatic family and appears in demanding performance combinations. C&C starts with the tag, donor history, and complete vehicle setup before choosing parts.

Why owners look for an 8HP90 upgrade

A high-power street or race car can add grip, launch load, heat, and repeated hard shifts. The peak number matters, but the torque curve and the way the car uses it matter too.

C&C looks at the transmission as part of the car: engine, torque curve, weight, tire, launch behavior, converter, TCU calibration, cooling, and current condition.

Dodson Motorsport product family

A dedicated 8HP90 and 8HP95 range

Dodson publishes a dedicated 8HP90 and 8HP95 upgrade range. Its official product family includes a SUPERSTOCK clutch kit and a billet P2 planetary carrier assembly. C&C determines what is relevant to the exact transmission and build.

What C&C looks at

  • Donor and recipient vehicle information
  • Engine combination, torque curve, and power goals
  • Vehicle weight, tire, launch strategy, and race frequency
  • Converter, TCU calibration, cooling, and driveline setup
  • Current condition, faults, service history, and prior work

What to have ready

  • A clear transmission tag photo
  • Part number or diagnostic identification, if available
  • Donor history when the unit came from another vehicle
  • Converter, TCU, and cooling details
  • Current symptoms, intended use, and goals for the car
