How a C&C 8HP Build Starts

A useful 8HP conversation does not need a perfect spec sheet. It needs a readable tag, a clear picture of the car, and an honest description of how it is used.

Start Your Build
  1. 01

    Tell C&C about the car

    Send the year, make, model, engine, drivetrain, current output, target output, tire, and how the car is used.

  2. 02

    Show the transmission

    Add a readable tag photo, donor history, mileage, symptoms, fault codes, service history, and any previous work.

  3. 03

    C&C recommends a direction

    C&C matches the parts discussion to the transmission, torque delivery, traction, controls, condition, and intended use.

  4. 04

    Review the quote

    The written quote is where the parts, price, timing, shipping instructions, and warranty terms become specific to the job.

Before the quote

C&C has to see the whole hit.

Weight, tire, torque delivery, launch behavior, converter, TCU calibration, cooling, and current condition can all change the parts discussion.
