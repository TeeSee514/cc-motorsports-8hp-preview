What is a ZF 8HP transmission?+

ZF 8HP refers to a family of eight-speed automatic transmissions used across multiple vehicles and variants. The family name is not enough to select parts or approve service; the exact transmission and application must be identified.

Which 8HP variants does C&C Motorsports support?+

C&C has not approved a public acceptance list. Candidate 8HP51, 8HP76, 8HP70, and 8HP75 applications shown on this development site require individual review and are not confirmed offerings.

How do I identify my exact transmission variant?+

Provide the vehicle VIN, transmission tag photographs, available part numbers, diagnostic identification, calibration details, and donor history. C&C must approve the final identification method and acceptance criteria.

How is a Street, Competition, or Max Effort package selected?+

Selection is provisional and application based. The review considers exact variant, vehicle, engine, drivetrain, current and target output and torque, fuel, forced induction, TCU strategy, tire, use, launch frequency, condition, symptoms, and existing upgrades.

How much horsepower can a complete C&C build hold?+

No complete-build horsepower rating is approved for publication. Component manufacturer ratings describe particular components under the manufacturer's conditions; they are not a C&C complete-transmission capacity and are not guarantees.

How much torque can a complete C&C build hold?+

No complete-build torque rating is approved for publication. Torque delivery, calibration, tire, vehicle mass, drivetrain, temperature, launch strategy, condition, and exact variant all matter.

Are Dodson Motorsport components used?+

ZF 8HP builds available using Dodson Motorsport transmission components. Exact component selection and C&C package inclusion remain application dependent and unapproved for public listing.

What is included in each package?+

No final included-component list is approved. Each package page shows provisional direction only; the written application quote must identify selected work, parts, exclusions, responsibilities, and change authorization.

Are clutch hubs or a billet P2 carrier required?+

That decision depends on the exact variant, inspection findings, intended use, and final parts path. Nothing on this site should be read as universal inclusion or interchangeability.

Is a torque converter included?+

Converter inspection, service, replacement, or upgrade is application dependent. No converter inclusion or stall specification is approved for any package.

Are fluid, filter, and pan included?+

The required fluid specification and quantity, filter, pan, and related service scope remain application dependent and must be stated in the written quote.

Do I need to supply a core?+

Core requirements and acceptance criteria have not been approved for publication. The answer may depend on whether the service is local, in-vehicle, customer-unit, exchange, or ship-in.

Is in-vehicle or local service available?+

Local and in-vehicle service availability is subject to C&C approval for the exact application, scope, scheduling, and facility workflow. Do not transport a vehicle or transmission without direct written coordination.

Can I ship a transmission to C&C?+

A ship-in path is subject to approval. Packaging, freight direction, insurance, risk of loss, receiving inspection, rejected-core handling, and return shipping must be defined before shipment.

What is the typical build time?+

No typical lead time is approved. Timing can depend on application acceptance, inspection findings, parts availability, current workload, change approvals, shipping, and any supporting service.

What does a build cost?+

Pricing is quoted by application. Starting prices, deposits, inspection fees, taxes, freight, core charges, payment schedule, and cancellation terms remain unapproved until C&C supplies written terms.

What warranty applies?+

No public ZF 8HP warranty terms are approved. Coverage, duration, exclusions, claim procedure, labor, shipping, calibration requirements, competition-use treatment, and transferability must be provided in writing before service authorization.

Is TCU calibration required or provided?+

Calibration requirements are application dependent. This development site does not claim that C&C supplies, installs, or supports a particular TCU product or calibration.

Does every modified vehicle need a built transmission?+

No. A stock 8HP is not automatically fragile, and need depends on the exact unit, condition, operating load, calibration, traction, use, and goals. The purpose of review is to avoid selecting from a single number or assumption.

What happens after I submit the build request?+

In the current staging dry-run, validated synthetic data is discarded immediately and no email or shop notification is sent. A live intake and response workflow must be approved and implemented before real customer submissions are accepted.
