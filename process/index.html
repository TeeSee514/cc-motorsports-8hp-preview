Four Steps From Tag Photo to Written Quote

A useful 8HP conversation does not need a perfect specification sheet. It needs a readable tag, the complete vehicle, and an honest description of the use.

  2. 02

  3. 03

    Parts, price, timing, shipping instructions, and warranty terms become specific here.

Before the quote

The transmission family is only the starting point.

Weight, tire, torque delivery, launch behavior, converter, TCU calibration, cooling, current condition, and actual use can all change the parts discussion.
